Nexa Warning Letter

Warning letters, issued, read and documented

HR issues the warning letter, the employee receives it by e-mail, reads it in Odoo and confirms it. Every letter is numbered, locked, printable and stays in the history.

Free Open source - LGPL-3 No external service

An issued warning letter: number, validity, details, watchers and the chatter

From the draft to the signature

Types with a validity

Documented verbal warning, warning letter 1, 2 and 3 are ready for each company. Each type fills the end date of the letter.

Numbered and locked

An issued letter gets its number (one numbering per company, no gap) and cannot be changed or deleted. Cancel it to correct it.

E-mail and watchers

The employee receives the letter by e-mail. The watchers, for example the manager, receive a copy and follow the comments.

Read and confirmed

Employees read their letters in My Warning Letters and confirm with one click. HR sees who has not read his letter yet.

Printable letter

A PDF warning letter with the company header and two signature boxes, in the language of the employee.

Private where it matters

Employees never see drafts, the letters of others or the internal note of HR. Their access is checked on the server.

All the letters at a glance

Filter by status, validity or "not read yet", group by type, department or employee, and analyse them in a pivot view.

What the employee sees

A clean reading view with the button I Have Read It, the PDF and the chatter to reply to HR.

A letter ready to sign

Print the warning letter with your company layout: reasons, validity and the signature boxes of HR and of the employee.

Sent by e-mail

The e-mail template can be edited. Switch the e-mail and the copy to the watchers on or off per company.

Active and total warning letters on the employee form

Settings per company: e-mail, copy, numbering and types

Good to know

  • Works with the standard Employees application of Odoo 19, Community and Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or on your own server.
  • Only adds menus, a button on the employee form and a settings block: the standard Employees application keeps working as before.
  • Roles: Employees Officer issues the letters, Employees Administrator also deletes them and manages the types, every employee reads his own letters.
  • No Python package to install and no external service. English and Indonesian included.
